Brain Machine Interface
Developed an end-to-end EEG-based brain–computer interface for electric wheelchair control, as measured by reliably mapping four locomotion commands (forward, left, right, stop) from frontal (F3) and parietal (P8) EEG channels into microcontroller-driven motor actions, by integrating an NIC2 8-channel headset, 8–31 Hz bandpass filtering, FFT + wavelet feature extraction, PCA-based feature compression, and Arduino-based wheelchair actuation 
Increased EEG command classification performance to 94.9% Mean Average Precision, as measured by Random Forest and XGBoost models outperforming baselines such as SVM, KNN, Logistic Regression, and Naive Bayes (all ≥80% MAP), by engineering wavelet-based cognitive features and systematically benchmarking six supervised classifiers plus ensemble methods across multiple train–test splits
Validated mind-controlled wheelchair locomotion with human subjects, as measured by decoding 200 EEG trials (4 commands × 10 repetitions × 5 participants with varying ages, genders, and physical disabilities) into accurate navigation decisions, by designing a visual-attention protocol (object-focused motor imagery with blink-based stop) and implementing a real-time EEG streaming, preprocessing, and classification interface for live wheelchair control 

Uncertainty-Aware Alignment
* Improved robustness of RLHF alignment under noisy / OOD reward signals, as measured by more stable policy updates with penalized high-uncertainty samples, by co-developing an uncertainty-aware preference distillationframework that builds a test-time reward ensemble via decoding-time realignment and combines lower-confidence-bound rewards with adaptive KL regularization based on Kendall Tau ranking consistency.  
* Boosted Llama3.2-3B instruction-following quality, as measured by raising AlpacaEval 2.0 length-controlled win rate from 27.15% → 29.76% and overall win rate from 30.31% → 32.98% over a standard DPO baseline, by implementing an ensemble RLHF pipeline that samples 4 completions per prompt, aggregates 3 reward variants, and plugs uncertainty-aware rewards into the DPO-style objective.  
* Validated generality across model families, as measured by improving Qwen2.5-7B AlpacaEval 2.0 length-controlled win rate to 41.80% and overall win rate to 41.62%, outperforming single-reward RLHF and DPO baselines, by running large-scale preference optimization and evaluation on Ultrafeedback-aligned models using a 4×H100 GPU setup.
